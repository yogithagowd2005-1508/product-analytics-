## 1. Matrix Overview & Governance
The **Product Data Coverage Matrix** serves as the authoritative operational mapping connecting user actions, system operations, and business consequences to concrete data attributes, event schemas, data sources, and analytical priorities across all 14 VELOOP product domains.

- **Primary Data File**: `deliverables_tables/03_DATA_COVERAGE_MATRIX.csv`
- **Total Product Modules Audited**: 20 distinct operational modules
- **Status Classification**: `Confirmed` (Core verified functionality) | `Proposed` (Architectural recommendation) | `To Be Verified` (Pending engineering confirmation)
- **Priority Tiering**: `Critical` (Blocks financial/core reporting) | `High` (Core product analytics) | `Medium` (Operational optimization) | `Low` (Exploratory)

## 3. Structural Analysis of Coverage

### Priority Distribution
- **Critical (8 modules / 40%)**: User Registration, Login, Video Ads, Daily Check-ins, Referee Activation, Token Exchange, Withdrawal Request, and Settlement. These modules form the financial spine and baseline retention telemetry.
- **High (7 modules / 35%)**: Onboarding, Surveys, Mining Claims, Spin Wheel, Missions, Referral Invites, and Withdrawal Fraud Review.
- **Medium (5 modules / 25%)**: Tap & Earn, Streak Protection, Badges/Levels, and Help Center Navigation.

### Data Source Breakdown
- **Mobile Client SDK**: Captures direct user interactions, button clicks, and UI lifecycle.
- **Core Backend Services**: Generates authoritative timestamps, user state transitions, and ledger credits.
- **Third-Party Partner Webhooks (S2S)**: Asynchronous postbacks for ad verification, surveys, and payment rail settlement.
